State courts may exercise concurrent jurisdiction over federal questions where Congress has not granted exclusive jurisdiction to federal courts. Practical necessity demands this: the federal judiciary cannot handle every case that touches federal law. State courts supplement federal capacity and, subject to Supreme Court review, provide an acceptable first-instance forum for many federal claims.
