Senate involvement in appointments promotes stability: the president cannot dismiss officers and replace them without Senate consent, preventing arbitrary and politically motivated turnover in the executive departments. Answers objections to the appointment process by showing that the Senate's role is purely confirmatory — initiative remains entirely with the president — while providing an institutional check against unfit nominees.
