Paper barriers alone cannot contain legislative encroachment. Each department must have actual constitutional means — not just parchment declarations — to resist the ambitions of the others. The legislature, drawing its power from the people, is the branch most likely to aggrandize itself; the constitution must arm the other departments to resist.
