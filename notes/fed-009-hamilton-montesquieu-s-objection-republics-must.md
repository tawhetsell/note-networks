Montesquieu's objection — that republics must be small — is answered by the compound republic. The science of politics has advanced: balanced government, legislative checks, independent judiciary, representation. A confederate republic (a union of republics) preserves republican liberty while achieving the security of a larger state. The extended sphere tames faction rather than aggravating it.
