Congress may structure inferior federal courts as it sees fit. Supreme Court appellate review of state court decisions on federal questions is necessary to maintain uniform constitutional interpretation. Hamilton argues that suits by individuals against states without consent are not authorized by Article III — a position partially refuted by Chisholm v. Georgia (1793), which prompted the Eleventh Amendment.
