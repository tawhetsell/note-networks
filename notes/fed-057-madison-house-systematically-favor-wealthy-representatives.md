The House will not systematically favor the wealthy. Representatives must stand for election, are chosen by all qualified voters, and are bound by the laws they make — they cannot exempt themselves. The operative check against aristocratic capture is equal suffrage: every voter has the same weight. The structural argument that the House will represent ordinary citizens, not just the privileged.
