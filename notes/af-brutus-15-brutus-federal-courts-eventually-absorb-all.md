Federal courts will eventually absorb all state judicial functions through expansion of the Article III 'arising under' jurisdiction. The practical scope of federal jurisdiction has no natural stopping point: any case touching a federal statute or constitutional right can be characterized as arising under federal law. State courts will be reduced to administrative tribunals reviewing purely local matters. Cited in debates over the scope of Article III and federal question jurisdiction.
