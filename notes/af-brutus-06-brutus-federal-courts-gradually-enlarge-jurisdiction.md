Federal courts will gradually enlarge their jurisdiction and give broad construction to every federal power. The distinction between cases arising under federal law and common-law cases will erode until federal courts exercise effectively general jurisdiction. State courts will be subordinated to federal review on virtually every question touching national law. Unlike legislative overreach, judicial overreach cannot be corrected at the ballot box. Brutus's prediction proved prescient for federal judicial expansion.
