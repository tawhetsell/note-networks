The Senate is the proper court for impeachment trials. Impeachment concerns political offenses — abuse of public trust — that a regular jury is unfit to judge. A political body, held accountable by the people, must judge political crimes. The Chief Justice presides to ensure procedural dignity and to prevent the Vice President from presiding over a trial that determines his own succession. Criminal prosecution after acquittal remains available; double jeopardy does not bar it.
