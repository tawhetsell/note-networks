Critics have wildly exaggerated the president's constitutional powers. Hamilton provides a precise inventory showing the executive is granted far less authority than opponents claim — and far less than a king. The vacancy-filling power for Senate seats, which critics attribute to the president, actually belongs to the states. Begins the systematic examination of executive powers running through #68–77.
