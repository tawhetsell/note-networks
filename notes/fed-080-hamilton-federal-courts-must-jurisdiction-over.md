Federal courts must have jurisdiction over all cases arising under the Constitution, federal statutes, and treaties; over controversies between states or citizens of different states; and over admiralty. Leaving federal questions to state courts would produce thirteen different interpretations of national law. Uniform judicial authority over the subjects of federal concern is essential to the meaning and enforceability of the Constitution.
