Presidential nomination with Senate confirmation is the best available mechanism for appointments. The president has incentive and superior information to nominate competent officers; the Senate can reject unfit nominees without bearing the burden of original selection. Congressional appointment invites logrolling and cabal; a single accountable executive's judgment is both more reliable and more transparent to the public.
