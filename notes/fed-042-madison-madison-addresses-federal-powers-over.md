Madison addresses federal powers over foreign intercourse and interstate relations. The commerce clause encompasses foreign trade, interstate commerce, and Indian trade under one coherent federal power — eliminating the conflicts and retaliations that plagued commerce under the Articles. The privileges and immunities clause ensures citizens receive equal treatment in every state. The law of nations, treaties, and extradition are brought under consistent federal authority. Cited by SCOTUS more times than any other paper in the series according to empirical citation studies.
