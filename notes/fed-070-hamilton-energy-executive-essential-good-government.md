Energy in the executive is essential to good government: protection from foreign attack, steady law enforcement, security against faction and anarchy. Energy requires unity — one executive, not a council. Unity ensures decision, dispatch, and accountability. A council diffuses responsibility; a single executive can be watched and judged by the people. A plural executive, far from being safer, hides responsibility behind collective decision.
