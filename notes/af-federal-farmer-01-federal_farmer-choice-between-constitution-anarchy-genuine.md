The choice is not between this Constitution and anarchy — genuine reform of the Articles was possible without replacement. The Federal Farmer urges deliberation over urgency: the Convention exceeded its mandate by proposing a new frame rather than amendments. Consolidation of power, once achieved, is difficult to reverse. A true federal system requires stronger state representation and a genuine balance between levels of government. Attributed to Richard Henry Lee or Melancton Smith.
