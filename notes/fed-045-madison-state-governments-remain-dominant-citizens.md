State governments will remain dominant. Citizens identify primarily with their states; state officers vastly outnumber federal officers; local concerns preoccupy most voters. The enumerated federal powers are few and defined; the residual state powers are numerous and indefinite. The sacrifice of state authority to the Union is real but modest compared to the benefits of peace, commerce, and collective defense.
