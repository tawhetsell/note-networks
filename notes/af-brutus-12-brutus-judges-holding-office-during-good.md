Judges holding office during good behavior, with fixed salaries and no political accountability, will become a law unto themselves. The power to void legislative acts makes the court superior to Congress, yet the court itself faces no equivalent check. The remedy — impeachment — is impractical for judicial overreach on constitutional questions. Cited in cases and scholarship on the countermajoritarian difficulty and judicial accountability.
