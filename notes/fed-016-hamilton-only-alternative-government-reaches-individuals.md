The only alternative to a government that reaches individuals directly is military coercion of states. Coercing a state as a political body means war; armies cannot distinguish innocent citizens from delinquent governments. A federal government that operates through courts and laws on individual persons avoids this fatal dilemma. The Constitution's design — federal law binding on individuals — is the structural solution. Cited in commandeering and anti-commandeering cases including Printz v. United States.
