A unified America can negotiate commercial treaties from strength. Divided into competing states, Europe plays American ports against each other, extracting favorable terms none could resist individually. Federal control of navigation and trade policy enables retaliatory measures that secure mutual respect. Hamilton envisions the United States eventually becoming a naval power through unified commercial policy — impossible under the Articles. Cited in commerce clause and federal trade power cases.
