The Articles of Confederation are fatally defective: federal law operates on states, not individuals. Law that cannot reach individuals must rely on state compliance, which is inconsistent. Armies and courts act on persons, not political bodies. This structural flaw requires replacing — not merely patching — the existing government. The Confederation is not a government at all; it is a treaty among sovereigns.
