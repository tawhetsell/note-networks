Hamilton closes the series by urging immediate ratification of the imperfect Constitution, with amendments to follow through Article V. Perfection is impossible; the real choice is between this Constitution and the chaos of a dissolved Union. A second constitutional convention would produce greater disagreement, not greater wisdom. Accept a good constitution now rather than chase a perfect one that will never arrive.
