Distinguishes republic — government by representatives — from pure democracy — government by citizens in assembly. America's vast territory is navigable by roads and rivers; representatives travel to the capital, not all citizens. The size objection is answered by the very nature of representation. Cited in cases addressing the scope of federal legislative power over large geographic areas.
