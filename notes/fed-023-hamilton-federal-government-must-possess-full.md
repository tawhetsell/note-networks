The federal government must possess full and unlimited power over national defense. The means of national security cannot be limited in advance because threats are unlimited and unpredictable. A government forbidden to raise armies adequate to the danger is no government at all. This is the foundational argument for a strong federal military establishment over reliance on state militias for national security.
