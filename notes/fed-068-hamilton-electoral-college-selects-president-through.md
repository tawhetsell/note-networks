The Electoral College selects the president through deliberative intermediaries — citizens chosen for judgment, insulated from popular passion and foreign intrigue. Electors meet separately in each state, preventing cabal. The body is temporary, created solely for the election, reducing corruption risk. No office more important to get right; no other feature more carefully designed.
