The federal government is more likely than individual states to honor treaties and avoid just causes for war. State governments, subject to local passions and particular interests, will be tempted to violate treaties for parochial advantage. A single national government with national perspective can apply consistent, dispassionate judgment to foreign relations. Cited in foreign affairs and treaty power cases.
