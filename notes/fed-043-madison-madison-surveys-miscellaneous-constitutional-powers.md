Madison surveys miscellaneous constitutional powers: the guarantee of republican government to every state, admission of new states, the federal district for the capital, Article V's amendment procedure, and Article VII's ratification rule. He defends the amendment process as appropriately balanced — neither so easy as to invite instability nor so difficult as to prevent necessary corrections.
