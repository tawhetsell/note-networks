Are the powers granted to the federal government appropriate and safe? Madison argues the general welfare clause does not confer unlimited legislative power — it is a summary heading for the specific enumerated powers that follow. Military and commercial powers are not dangerous grants but necessities: a government that cannot defend its territory or regulate commerce is not a government. The question is not whether powers might be abused but whether they are indispensable.
