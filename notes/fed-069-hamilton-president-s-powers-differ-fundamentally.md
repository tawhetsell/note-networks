The president's powers differ fundamentally from a monarch's: four-year limited term, no hereditary succession, suspensive rather than absolute veto, commander-in-chief subject to congressional control, no treaty power without Senate consent, no declaration of war. Systematic comparison to the British Crown and the New York governor shows the Constitution's executive is republican, not monarchical.
