Answers specific objections to the Senate as impeachment court: the Senate will not simultaneously be judge and legislator in any meaningful sense — its judicial function in impeachment is entirely distinct from its legislative role. The President will not escape accountability through the Senate's foreign-affairs role; the two functions are separate. Hamilton systematically disposes of each procedural objection to the Senate trial.
