State officers take oaths to support the federal Constitution; they administer federal law at the local level. Regular operation of the federal government through and alongside state governments will build popular attachment to federal authority. Federal law reaches individuals directly — it does not depend on state mediation. This integration of state and federal government ensures allegiance from both directions.
