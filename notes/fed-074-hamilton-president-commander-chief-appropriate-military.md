The president as commander-in-chief is appropriate: military command demands unity and decision that divided authority cannot provide. The pardon power properly rests in a single hand — a legislative pardon would invite political manipulation. In moments of rebellion, the executive's ability to extend clemency without conditions can restore order more effectively than continued prosecution.
