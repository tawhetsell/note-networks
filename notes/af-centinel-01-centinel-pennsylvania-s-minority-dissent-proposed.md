Pennsylvania's minority dissent. The proposed Constitution shifts power to a distant, aristocratic central government accountable to no one. A single accountable legislature is superior to bicameralism, which insulates aristocratic senators from popular pressure. The Convention operated in secrecy; its product was rushed to ratification without adequate public debate. Haste benefits those who profit from confusion. Attributed to Samuel Bryan.
