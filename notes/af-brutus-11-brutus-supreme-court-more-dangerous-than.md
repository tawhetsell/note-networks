The Supreme Court is more dangerous than any other branch because its decisions are final and unreviewable. Judges will give the most liberal construction to congressional powers, and their interpretations will harden into binding precedent impossible to reverse. Unlike Britain, where Parliament can override court decisions, the proposed Constitution provides no mechanism to check judicial overreach. This essay is the Anti-Federalist paper most frequently cited in modern SCOTUS opinions — often invoked against judicial supremacy by justices across the ideological spectrum.
