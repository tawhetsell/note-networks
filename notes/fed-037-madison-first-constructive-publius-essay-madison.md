The first constructive Publius essay: Madison marvels at the Convention's achievement. The delegates faced unprecedented difficulty — blending national and federal elements, balancing energy with liberty, stability with accountability, using political language too imprecise for so novel a task. No human document is perfect; the Constitution should be judged against realistic alternatives, not an impossible standard of perfection.
