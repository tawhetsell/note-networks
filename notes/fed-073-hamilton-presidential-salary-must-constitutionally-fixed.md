Presidential salary must be constitutionally fixed — neither increased nor decreased during a term — to preserve independence from both Congress and the states. The qualified veto is defensive: the president can protect executive functions from legislative encroachment and prevent hasty legislation from becoming law. The two-thirds override requirement balances executive caution against majority legislative will. Cited in cases addressing the scope and limits of presidential veto power.
